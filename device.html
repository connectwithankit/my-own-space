NOVANAVA Working title · Early-stage concept

A dedicated voice companion device

A physical home
for personal AI.

We're building a small, dedicated device that puts a personal AI assistant one button away. Press, speak, get an answer.

One button.No phone to unlock.No screen.

NovaNava (working title) is our concept for screen-free access to personal AI. We're starting with a focused promise: make talking to an assistant easier.

01 / The device

Mic. Speaker. One button.

Our first design centers on push-to-talk: a microphone to listen, a speaker to respond, and a button to start the conversation. The device is the ear and mouth; the AI does the reasoning.

02 / Why now

Less friction, more conversation.

Our thesis is that access is now a bigger barrier than conversation quality. We want to remove the unlock-and-open-app routine. Our prototype plan combines ESP32-class hardware, ESPHome, and a 3D-printed enclosure.

03 / Who we're building for

People who already use AI daily.

We're starting with AI power users who want an assistant within reach at their desk, in the kitchen, or in the workshop. A dedicated voice device should fit into the space, without asking for another screen.

04 / Claude's role

The reasoning layer.

We plan to use Claude for reasoning and multi-step tasks, with a connected software layer handling speech and personal context. Our hardware stays simple: a way to speak to the assistant and hear its response.

05 / Our bet

Do one thing well.

We're not trying to replace the phone. We're building around one interaction: talking to a personal AI assistant without picking it up. Our advantage must come from focus, everyday usefulness, and an honest promise about what the device can do.
